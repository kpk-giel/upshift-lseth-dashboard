Running at 66% LTV — the top of the pre-automation band; ~75% is next
The position is held in band automatically: live rebalancing and anti-liquidation automations re-lever on strength and de-risk on weakness around this level. As they build a longer track record the target moves to ~75%, where the same structure yields +2.46% on the current spread — drag the slider above to see any point in between. Position size is a deliberately small pilot: the carry is a percentage, so it is the same at this size or at scale.
Backtest cross-check Over the 180 days to 30 Jul 2026, on reconstructed on-chain borrow rates and the vault's realized share price, this strategy at 75% LTV averaged +2.11% net over lsETH — monthly range 1.32% to 3.17%, with the 1.5% floor cleared on 75% of days. That run used deeper 86%-LLTV proxy markets, because lsETH/USDC had no rate history at the time; its own 45 days since have averaged 4.01% borrow at 89% utilization, against the proxy's 4.01%.
